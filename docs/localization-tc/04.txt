151	<strong>Staking Nodes</strong>
152	Staking nodes participate in network security and consensus by <strong>staking MHA</strong> to <strong>validate blocks</strong> and <strong>vote</strong>. Rewards are distributed based on <strong>staking amount × uptime/performance</strong>. Delegators can delegate their tokens to validators to share in the rewards.
153	<strong>Ecosystem / DAO Treasury</strong>
154	Allocated for <strong>developer grants, ecosystem partnerships, hackathons, community budgets</strong>, and other ecosystem-driven initiatives, subject to <strong>DAO proposal approval</strong> and <strong>milestone-based disbursements</strong>. <strong>Unmet milestones will revert back</strong> to the treasury.
155	Annual disbursements will not exceed <strong>10% of the total supply</strong>.
156	<strong>Implementation Method:</strong>
157	48-month linear release + multi-signature approval + time lock + quarterly transparency reports.
158	<strong>Team &amp; Advisors</strong>
159	Allocated for <strong>long-term incentives for core team members</strong> and <strong>advisor compensation</strong>.
160	<strong>Implementation Method:</strong>
161	<strong>18-month cliff</strong> followed by <strong>120-month linear vesting</strong>.
162	<strong>Clawback</strong> provision applies in case of resignation or failure to meet performance goals, allowing unvested tokens to be reclaimed.
163	<strong>VC (Investors, Institutional/Private Round)</strong>
164	Allocated for <strong>early-stage contributions from institutional investors</strong> or <strong>qualified investors</strong>.
165	<strong>Implementation Method:</strong>
166	<strong>12-month cliff</strong> followed by <strong>24-month linear vesting</strong>.
167	<strong>Early Supporters</strong>
168	Allocated for individuals or groups who were the <strong>earliest to engage in community building</strong>, <strong>beta testing</strong>, <strong>content contribution</strong>, and <strong>node setup</strong>.
169	<strong>Implementation Method:</strong>
170	<strong>6-month cliff</strong>, followed by <strong>1/6 release in the 7th month</strong>, and the remaining <strong>5/6 vesting over 24 months</strong>.
171	Introduce <strong>Proof of Contribution (PoC)</strong> to prevent "airdrop hunters" and ensure rewards are tied to actual contributions.

193	According to the MHA tokenomics, 3% of the total supply is allocated to public sales, which will be completed before listing. All tokens purchased during this period will be listed on global top-10 centralized exchanges. After the listing, these tokens will be locked for six months, with linear vesting beginning in the seventh month.

195	<strong>Public Sale OG (1%):</strong> Prior to the listing, an early-supporter public sale will be conducted at the lowest public-sale price; at the time of listing the token price will not be below this level. Tokens will be locked for six months after listing and then released continuously over twenty-four months.
196	**KOL/ECO Sale (0.5%):**The public sale for KOLs and ecosystem partners will be priced higher than the Public Sale OG. Tokens will be locked for six months after listing and then released continuously over twenty-four months.
197	**Public Sale Session 1 (1.5%):**The formal pre-sale will be open to the public worldwide at a price higher than that of the Public Sale OG. Tokens will be locked for six months after listing and then released continuously over twenty-four months.
