1201	FAQs
1202	MAGNE Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1203	<strong>What are M Hash L2's performance metrics?</strong>
1204	M Hash L2 currently offers:
1205	Block time: ~2 seconds
1206	TPS: thousands of transactions per second depending on gas usage and block size
1207	Finality: Single Slot Finality for fast and secure confirmations.
1208	<strong>What is a DEX?</strong>
1209	A DEX (Decentralized Exchange) is a protocol that enables on-chain token swaps entirely through smart contracts, without relying on a centralized intermediary.
1210	<strong>What is a swap?</strong>
1211	A swap is the process of exchanging one token for another — for example, swapping 
1212	MHA means selling USDC and buying MHA.
1213	<strong>How much does it cost to swap?</strong>
1214	Swap fees vary depending on the pool and are typically 0.05%, 0.1%, 0.3%, or 1%. Always check the fee before executing a swap.
1215	<strong>What is liquidity?</strong>
1216	Liquidity refers to the amount of a token available for swapping. Higher liquidity = lower slippage = better pricing.
1217	<strong>What is a liquidity pool?</strong>
1218	A liquidity pool consists of two or more tokens deposited by users, enabling token swaps on the DEX.
1219	<strong>What is a liquidity provider?</strong>
1220	Liquidity providers (LPs) are users who deposit tokens into liquidity pools. They earn a share of the trading fees generated in those pools.
1221	<strong>What is APY?</strong>
1222	APY stands for Annual Percentage Yield — the yearly rate of return from providing liquidity, mostly generated from swap fees.
1223	<strong>What is $MHA?</strong>
1224	$MHA is the native and only token of the MAGNE network. It powers gas, staking, governance, and incentives.
1225	Total supply is fixed.
1226	<strong>15% of the total supply</strong> is allocated to validator and staking rewards.
1227	Emissions follow a 
1228	halving schedule
1229	Year 1: 7.5% released
1230	Year 2: 3.75% released
1231	Year 3: 1.875% released
1232	And so on.
1233	This ensures long-term sustainability and controlled token inflation.
1234	<strong>Does MAGNE use a dual-token system?</strong>
1235	No. MAGNE uses a <strong>single-token model</strong> where $MHA powers gas, staking, incentives, and governance.
1236	<strong>Can anyone become a validator?</strong>
1237	Yes. Anyone can become a validator, but they must meet both staking requirements and node hardware standards to ensure network security and stability.
1238	<strong>How are validators selected?</strong>
1239	Validators are not determined by a fixed number of nodes but by <strong>the total amount of $MHA staked across the network</strong>.
1240	The number of active validators will be determined through governance and community discussions.
1241	Reward distribution is based on the <strong>proportion of tokens staked</strong> by each validator relative to the total network stake.
1242	If there are few nodes, the reward model can be dynamically adjusted, with a Rank-based incentive mechanism applied if needed.
1243	<strong>What are the staking requirements for validators?</strong>
1244	Minimum stake per validator: <strong>250,000 $MHA</strong>
1245	Maximum stake per validator: <strong>10,000,000 $MHA</strong> (subject to governance changes)
1246	Higher stake = higher chance of being in the active validator set and receiving a larger share of rewards.
1247	<strong>How are validator rewards distributed?</strong>
1248	Validator rewards come from the 15% emission pool, halving annually.
1249	Reward share per validator = (Validator stake ÷ Total network stake) × annual emission.
1250	When the number of validators is low, distribution may be adjusted using Rank incentives to favor high-quality nodes.
1251	<strong>What are the node hardware requirements?</strong>
1252	Validators must meet minimum technical standards to maintain stable and secure operations, including but not limited to:
1253	Reliable server infrastructure
1254	High availability and low network latency
1255	Adequate CPU, RAM, storage, and bandwidth
1256	High uptime to ensure block production and finality
1257	Only nodes that meet these standards are eligible for validator rewards.
1258	<strong>Can the number of validators change over time?</strong>
1259	Yes. The number of validators is <strong>dynamic</strong> and depends on total network stake, governance decisions, and network growth. In early stages with fewer validators, reward allocation will be adjusted to maintain fair incentives and network security.
1260	<strong>What is the Rank incentive mechanism?</strong>
1261	The Rank incentive mechanism ensures high-performance nodes receive greater rewards when validator numbers are low or distribution is imbalanced.
1262	Validators are ranked based on uptime, performance, and block production.
1263	Higher rank = higher reward weight.
1264	This keeps the network stable and encourages quality node operation.
1265	<strong>Do I automatically earn rewards after providing liquidity?</strong>
1266	No. You must stake your LP token into the corresponding Reward Vault to earn PoL (Proof-of-Liquidity) rewards. Rewards are accumulated and must be claimed manually.
1267	<strong>How do I earn PoL rewards?</strong>
1268	Provide liquidity and receive an LP token.
1269	Stake the LP token into a whitelisted Reward Vault.
1270	If a validator allocates emissions to that Vault, you earn $MHA over time.
1271	Claim your rewards manually from your wallet.
1272	<strong>Who can vote or submit proposals?</strong>
1273	Any $MHA holder can participate in governance voting.
1274	Proposal submission may require holding a minimum amount of $MHA (e.g., 10,000 tokens).
1275	Validator set size, node requirements, and emission parameters can be updated through governance.
1276	<strong>Can Vaults route emissions to specific pools?</strong>
1277	Yes. Each Reward Vault corresponds to a specific receipt token (e.g., LP token). dApps can request Vaults for particular pools, and validators can allocate emissions to boost targeted liquidity.
1278	<strong>What is the overall validator incentive model?</strong>
1279	MAGNE’s validator incentive design is based on:
1280	<strong>Annual halving</strong> of emissions
1281	<strong>Stake-weighted reward distribution</strong>
1282	<strong>Dynamic validator set size</strong>
1283	<strong>Rank-based adjustments for quality performance</strong>
1284	This model ensures strong early incentives, sustainable long-term rewards, and network stability.
