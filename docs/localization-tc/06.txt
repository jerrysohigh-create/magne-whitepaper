278	Governance Overview
279	MAGNE Governance
280	MAGNE’s decentralized governance system empowers $MHA token holders to steer protocol-level changes including Reward Vault approvals, native dApp configurations, and PoW distribution logic.
281	Use Cases for Governance:
282	Whitelisting Reward Vaults (e.g. staking/incentive token approvals)
283	Configuring native dApps like MAGNESwap (e.g. fee structure)
284	Adjusting PoL distribution curves or parameters
285	Upgrading system-level settings or timelock durations
286	Governance Lifecycle
287	MAGNE’s governance is based on OpenZeppelin’s battle-tested contracts, providing a secure and transparent multi-phase lifecycle:
288	<strong>Proposal Creation</strong>
289	Any account with sufficient voting power may initiate a proposal
290	<strong>Pending State</strong>
291	Proposals enter a brief 1-hour delay before becoming active for voting
292	<strong>Voting Phase</strong>
293	$MHA holders (or their delegates) can cast votes during the 5-day voting window
294	<strong>Proposal Outcome</strong>
295	If quorum and majority thresholds are met, the proposal is marked as Succeeded
296	<strong>Timelock Queue</strong>
297	Successful proposals enter a 2-day delay queue before execution
298	<strong>Execution</strong>
299	Once timelock elapses, the change can be implemented on-chain by any actor
300	Guardian Oversight
301	To protect the protocol from malicious behavior, MAGNE uses a 5-of-9 Guardian Council with the authority to cancel dangerous proposals during the timelock phase (e.g., unauthorized upgrades or asset seizure).
302	Creating Your Governance Proposal
303	<strong>Ensure You Meet the Voting Threshold</strong>
304	You need at least <strong>10,000 $MHA worth of delegated voting power</strong>
305	<strong>Delegate to Yourself</strong>
306	You must explicitly delegate $MHA (even to yourself) to unlock governance rights
307	<strong>Prepare the Proposal Payload</strong>
308	For example, a Reward Vault proposal must include the vault address and correct function call (e.g., <span>addRewardVault(address)</span>)
309	<strong>Submit the Proposal</strong>
310	Submit through the on-chain governance contract or via the MAGNEHub UI
311	<strong>Promote the Proposal</strong>
312	Engage the community to build awareness and gather votes
313	<strong>Execute After Timelock</strong>
314	If passed, any user can trigger execution after the 2-day delay
