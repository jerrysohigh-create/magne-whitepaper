329	Incentive Marketplace
330	In the M Hash Layer2 PoW ecosystem, the Incentive Marketplace allows protocols to offer whitelisted incentive tokens to miners, encouraging them to support specific on-chain activities or liquidity pools. This creates a competitive environment where different protocols vie for miner attention and block reward allocation.
331	How Incentives Work
332	When a miner produces a block, they can choose to allocate part of their earned $MHA rewards toward one or more whitelisted Reward Vaults. These vaults are tied to specific protocols or applications.
333	When rewards are distributed:
334	The miner receives a commission in incentive tokens from the protocol
335	The remaining incentive tokens in the vault are distributed to participants who have staked eligible assets in that vault
336	All claims are processed automatically via M Hash Layer2 Hub
337	Users can claim their incentives at any time, with no expiration
338	Roles in the Incentive Marketplace
339	Role
340	Description
341	Participant
342	Stakes eligible assets (e.g., LP tokens, NFTs) into a Reward Vault to earn incentives
343	Miner
344	Produces blocks via PoW and directs part of $MHA rewards to selected vaults in exchange for incentives
345	Protocol
346	Provides incentive tokens to attract miner-directed rewards and increase engagement/liquidity
347	Incentive Token Governance
348	Only whitelisted Reward Vaults may distribute incentive tokens
349	Each vault can support up to two different incentive tokens
350	Adding a token or changing its manager requires governance approval

372	Commission Distribution
373	Miners can set a commission rate (default 5%, max 20%) for incentives captured from vaults
374	Example: If a vault pays 100 USDC for 10

376	Timing vs Rate
377	Distribution timing (e.g., 3-day or 7-day streaming) is set by the Reward Vault
378	The exchange rate (e.g., 10 USDC/$MHA) remains fixed regardless of the reward release schedule
