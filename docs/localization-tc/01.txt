2	What is <strong>MAGNE.AI</strong>?
3	<strong>MAGNE.AI is a next-generation blockchain platform that merges AI compute infrastructure, DePin networks, and Web3 applications. We aim to build a mobile-native, embedded, and autonomous AI-powered Web3 world.</strong>
4	The project is powered by two foundational technologies:
5	<strong>MAGNE Layer1 Blockchain</strong>: A mobile-first, EVM-compatible, PoW-based Layer1 chain where smartphones and edge devices can participate in mining and consensus, ensuring security, decentralization, and performance.
6	<strong>M Hash Layer2 Blockchain</strong>: A high-performance Layer 2 network built on opStack with an asynchronous consensus mechanism, separating consensus from execution to significantly boost throughput and efficiency while ensuring security and low-cost transactions.
7	<strong>MAGNE Kit Modular Execution Framework</strong>: A re-engineered execution layer optimized for low-latency smart contract processing, tailored for mobile use and AI-native workloads.
8	MAGNE.AI introduces a novel architecture that fuses AI compute with Web3 infrastructure:
9	Users earn $MHA tokens by sharing idle compute from devices like the <strong>MAGNE.AI Phone Gen1.</strong>
10	Builds a <strong>DePin (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure)</strong> network where edge devices act as nodes.
11	Provides AI APIs and SDKs for developers to build decentralized, AI-integrated applications.
12	Enables on-chain deployment, validation, pricing, and usage of AI models—realizing Web3-native AI-as-a-Service.
13	The MAGNE.AI ecosystem consists of:
14	<strong>Base infrastructure</strong>: MAGNE Layer1, M Hash Layer2
15	<strong>Device matrix</strong>: AI-powered phones, earbuds, and glasses (planned)
16	<strong>Application layer</strong>: Dapp Hub (Shin Getter Nexus), AI assistants, Web3 games, NFT platforms
17	<strong>Unified token economy</strong>: $MHA token used for gas, governance, incentives, staking, and access
18	MAGNE.AI redefines the boundary between “smart devices” and “decentralized infrastructure,” enabling trusted AI execution, traceable model provenance, data ownership, and multi-device collaboration. Through tight integration of protocol and hardware, MAGNE.AI empowers every user to become a contributor and beneficiary in the AI Web3 era.
