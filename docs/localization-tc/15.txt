805	MAGNE Layer1 Blockchain
806	<strong>Build an App</strong>
807	A guide to building a next.js app on MAGNE Layer1 Blockchain using OnchainKit
808	Welcome to the MAGNE Layer1 Blockchain quickstart guide! In this walkthrough, we'll create a simple onchain app from start to finish. Whether you're a seasoned developer or just starting out, this guide has got you covered.
809	<strong>What You'll Achieve</strong>
810	By the end of this quickstart, you'll have built an onchain app by:
811	Configuring your development environment
812	Deploying your smart contracts to MagicalHash
813	Interacting with your deployed contracts from the frontend
814	Our simple app will be an onchain tally app which lets you add to a total tally, stored onchain, by pressing a button.
815	Why MAGNE Layer1 Blockchain?
816	MAGNE Layer1 Blockchain is a mobile-first, EVM-compatible, PoW-based Layer1 chain where smartphones and edge devices can participate in mining and consensus, ensuring security, decentralization, and performance.
