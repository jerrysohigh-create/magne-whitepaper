IV

THE INTELLIGENCE

The cloud assists.
The device retains local intelligence.

MAG1 doesn’t lump all AI into one slogan. The system assistant, client-side language capabilities, and image computing each take their own paths and work together when they need to meet.

THREE PATHS / ONE DEVICE
01 / CLOUD AI
Google Gemini

As the default system AI assistant for overseas versions, it integrates side keys for quick access and Google cloud capabilities.

DEFAULT SYSTEM ASSISTANT
02 / ON-DEVICE AI
Qwen · ASR · TTS

Local Qwen2.5 1.5B cooperates with end-side speech recognition and synthesis to undertake translation and speech workflow.

LOCAL TRANSLATION
03 / DEDICATED NPU
IMG AX3596

A discrete NPU provides hardware acceleration for on-device inference; imaging AI is handled by a dedicated ISP and Vision path.
