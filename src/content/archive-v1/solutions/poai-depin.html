POAi and DePIN Integration: The Edge Revolution of MAGNE.AI

MAGNE.AI Phone Gen1 is more than just a smartphone—it is a node in the global distributed AI and blockchain network. By integrating POAi (Proof of AI) with DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks), MAGNE.AI transforms every device into both a blockchain validator and an AI computing contributor.

1. AI Computation Contribution (POAi)

  • Users can contribute idle device computing power to perform AI tasks, such as inference, model training, or data analytics
  • Contributions are transparent, verifiable, and recorded on-chain
  • Every computation generates a Proof of AI, which rewards users with $MHA tokens

2. Participation in DePIN Networks

  • Every MAGNE.AI device becomes an edge node in the DePIN ecosystem, supporting decentralized storage, bandwidth sharing, and IoT connectivity
  • No need for centralized data centers—millions of devices collectively form a global infrastructure
  • Users not only use the phone but also become part of the infrastructure layer and share in the rewards

3. Convergence of Blockchain and Hardware

  • Blockchain ensures transparent settlement and incentives
  • Hardware ensures secure computation and trusted execution
  • AI + DePIN turn devices from mere terminals into core infrastructure nodes

4. Global Significance

With POAi + DePIN, MAGNE.AI integrates fragmented device resources into a global-scale infrastructure:

  • AI training and inference can be executed across millions of devices in parallel
  • Decentralized networks no longer rely on a handful of large nodes but are built by user devices collectively
  • Users are not only consumers but also producers and contributors

MAGNE.AI is not just a phone—it is the gateway to distributed AI and decentralized infrastructure.
