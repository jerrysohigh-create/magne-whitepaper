Gas and fees

Gas measures execution work. A transaction fee is not the same as its gas limit or the quoted gas price.

Fee components

In the proposed L2 design, the fee includes execution gas plus the applicable cost of publishing data to L1. The data component must be estimated through the deployed fee mechanism; it is not obtained by reusing the L2 execution gas quantity. Any additional fee component must be disclosed with its implementation version.

L2 total fee = execution gas used × effective gas price + applicable L1 data fee

Base fee example

For an EIP-1559 block limit of 100M, elasticity of 2 and denominator of 8, the target is 50M gas. At the target the base fee remains unchanged; a full block produces approximately a 12.5% increase. These are illustrative values, not confirmed live settings.

Before submitting a transaction

  • Confirm the network and request an estimate from its RPC and wallet.
  • BSC MHA transfers require BNB gas; MAGNE testnets use their own test gas assets.
  • Batching and compression can reduce per-transaction data costs. ZK proofs are not asserted here as an enabled MAGNE fee-reduction mechanism.
