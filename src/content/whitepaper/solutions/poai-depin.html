PoAI and DePIN participation

The design explores distributed device computation with verifiable task results. PoAI is used here as a project design term for AI-work verification, not as a claim that useful AI work automatically proves blockchain consensus.

Task lifecycle

  • Define the task, input permissions, workload reference and acceptance criteria.
  • Assign eligible devices and collect results with time and device evidence.
  • Validate quality and detect duplicate, fabricated or ineligible work before settlement.

Device network

DePIN describes a network of contributed physical infrastructure. Devices sold, registered devices, online devices and accepted-task contributors are different counts. This chapter does not assert an active network of millions of devices.

Rewards and activation

GEN1 reward budgeting and release are specified in Tokenomics. The task catalogue, benchmark, verification method and activation date still require publication. Device participation is not an unconditional income entitlement.
