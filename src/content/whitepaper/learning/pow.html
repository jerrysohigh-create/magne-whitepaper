Proof of Work and device rewards

What PoW means

Proof of Work (PoW) is a consensus approach in which participants perform computational work to compete for block production under the rules of a specific chain. Computational task completion, on its own, is not proof that a device participates in that consensus.

How v1.1 uses the term mining

GEN1 mining refers to the proposed distribution of the Mining Nodes allocation for eligible device availability and effective tasks. Its baseline, monthly budget, work checks and release schedule are defined in Tokenomics. It is not a per-phone block reward and does not imply that every device is a full node or validator.

Consensus specification

Earlier material mixed PoW, CometBFT and staking-based validator descriptions. This review draft does not treat those descriptions as one confirmed implementation. Publication of the final L1 consensus model requires the client version, validator-selection rules, finality conditions and security assumptions. L2 ordering and settlement must be specified separately.
