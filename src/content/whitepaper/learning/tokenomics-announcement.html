Tokenomics v1.1 update

2 October 2026 · full-text reconciliation

This editorial update corrects incentive arithmetic, fee explanations, developer examples and cross-chapter status descriptions. It removes unsupported sale-price/listing assurances, identifies the unresolved sale-vesting descriptions and separates device rewards from consensus. Governance and mBGT design decisions remain pending. It does not change total allocation, allocation percentages, GEN1 calculation parameters or the Ecosystem / DAO budget limits, and does not execute any transfer.

Ecosystem budget and historical-expense reimbursement proposal

The 1.5 billion MHA pool retains equal release over 48 months at 31.25 million per month; individual project and campaign payments do not each receive a new 48-month schedule. The annual disbursement ceiling changes from the former wording of 10% of total supply to 10% of the original ecosystem allocation, or 150 million MHA, per consecutive 12-month year from the pool start date. It continues after pool release ends; unspent amounts do not increase later annual ceilings. A separate proposal assigns 1,635,000 MHA of historical community expenses to ecosystem budget and reimburses the Early Supporters reserve. The project confirms no reimbursement transfer as of 1 October 2026. Original airdrop records and snapshots remain intact; reimbursement requires reconciliation, approval and disclosure, counts toward the execution-year ceiling and creates neither a new user airdrop nor a change to Early Supporters vesting. These are proposed budget-rule changes and pending execution, not completed fund movements.

October 2026: GEN1 reward proposal

Adds a 13,750-device full-budget baseline, proportional budget reduction below that baseline and a proposed 20% availability / 80% useful-work split. Total supply, all ten top-level allocations and the 400,000,000 MHA GEN1 ceiling remain unchanged. This is a substantive reward-rule proposal, not just an editorial clarification, and is not effective yet. The old pool-exhausting ECU formula and repeated uptime deductions do not apply to the base illustration; locking/staking incentives are proposed separately with their own budget.

Adds 36 monthly earned, released and locked balances plus the M37–M38 vesting tail. For a fixed, equal-device cohort at or below the baseline, with full availability and 100% reference work throughout, cumulative release is 20,000 MHA at M24 and 23,818.18 MHA at M36. These are conditional calculations, not guaranteed receipts after purchase. Activation conditions and an effective date require a separate announcement.

GEN1 optional incentive budget proposal

Retains the base model and proposes, subject to funding verification and authorization, up to 17 million MHA from the 20 million initial-ramp budget difference. M3–M36 permits at most 500,000 in newly approved incentives monthly. Supply and top-level allocations are unchanged, but spending beyond the base curve is a substantive economic-rule change.
