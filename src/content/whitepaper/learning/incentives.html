Incentive Marketplace — design draft

This proposal describes how participating protocols could fund incentives for eligible reward vaults. It is separate from GEN1 device rewards and is not evidence of a live marketplace or L2 PoW mining. Activation requires confirmed contracts, eligible allocators and published vault rules.

Proposed flow

  • A protocol deposits incentive tokens into an approved vault.
  • An eligible allocator directs an authorised reward amount to the vault; the source allocation and spending authority must be documented.
  • Commission and participant awards are calculated from the funded incentive amount, subject to vault eligibility and available balance.

Roles

RoleResponsibility
ProtocolFunds incentives and proposes vault terms.
Reward allocatorExercises allocation authority under the final protocol rules; not automatically every miner or phone owner.
ParticipantProvides eligible assets and claims awards under the vault rules.

Draft vault controls

The earlier design proposed whitelisted vaults, up to two incentive tokens per vault, and governance approval for token or manager changes. Deposit withdrawal restrictions, rate-change rules, claim deadlines and any automated claims must be confirmed in the deployed contract terms before participation.

Commission example

At 100 USDC per 10 MHA, directing 1 MHA corresponds to 10 USDC of incentives. At a 5% commission, the allocator receives 0.5 USDC and participants share 9.5 USDC. The earlier proposed default of 5% and maximum of 20% remain design parameters, not active rates.

Release and claims

A vault may propose a streaming or staged release schedule. A quoted rate, a funded balance and a claimable award are different quantities. The final contract must specify eligibility, release timing, rounding and expiry; this draft does not promise unlimited claim time.
