Mining Nodes

Mining Nodes retain 3,000,000,000 MHA (30% of total supply). This section proposes a GEN1 device reward mechanism for verified availability and useful work. It does not establish that phone mining, settlement contracts or MAGNE mainnet are live.

Start with device count, then choose your schedule

  • Up to 13,750 eligible devices: the base mining budget scales with device count, so the per-device reference reward is the same under equal participation conditions. Use the standard schedule below for continuous eligibility, full availability and full reference work.
  • More than 13,750 eligible devices: the base mining budget stops growing with device count, and the per-device reference reward decreases as more devices participate. Use the reward calculator to generate the matching monthly schedule.
  • Changing monthly counts or less than 100% availability/work: adjust the scenario in the calculator even when counts are below the baseline. New devices affect newly earned rewards only; previously approved batches are not recalculated.

The 13,750-device baseline divides budget regimes; it is not a minimum-return threshold. The table assumes equal devices participating from program M1 with full verified availability and reference work. Late entrants cannot apply its full 24/36-month totals directly.

Voluntary locking: one award per batch

Select all or part of a newly approved monthly base reward, before any of that selected portion is claimed. Locking 6 / 9 / 12 months gives a request ceiling of 10% / 15% / 20% of the selected amount. A 500 MHA selection locked for 12 months requests at most 100 MHA, regardless of the device’s other rewards. The selected portion replaces normal vesting with release in full at maturity. Unselected base rewards keep their original vesting. Existing batches are not retroactively relocked, and the same principal cannot earn a second locking award.

The term runs from the accepted lock execution timestamp. No early withdrawal is modeled. Locking is voluntary and occurs only after the user accepts the final amount, vesting schedule and maturity date. A twelve-month lock receives one incentive for that batch, not twelve monthly interest payments.

Device staking: proposed initial tier

Bind a separate 3,000 MHA deposit to one eligible device. It qualifies from the next settlement month after the deposit is completed, subject to verification, with a monthly request ceiling of 10% of that device’s actual base reward. The same deposit cannot support multiple devices or also serve as voluntarily locked reward principal. Staking without an eligible device or without base rewards creates no reward.

A withdrawal request stops new staking incentive accrual from its timestamp; the final period must be prorated under published settlement rules. Principal is returned after 30 days. No automatic principal slashing is introduced by this proposal. Invalid evidence may affect unapproved rewards under a disclosed review and appeal process. The calculator assumes full-month eligibility and excludes this separate principal’s return.

No stacking; budget-limited quotes

Qᵢ = max(Lᵢ × bᵢ, Eᵢ × sᵢ)
k = min(1, B / ΣQᵢ)
Xᵢ = Qᵢ × k

E is approved base reward; L is the proposed voluntary-lock amount, capped by E; b is 10%, 15% or 20%; s is 10% for an eligible stake, otherwise zero. Non-selected or ineligible options count as zero. Each device uses one option, taking the higher request without addition or multiplication; ties select staking. B is the smaller of remaining monthly budget (at most 500,000) and remaining authorized program budget (within 17 million). If total requests or available budget are zero, no extra award is made.

Use a published cutoff and deduplicated request snapshot to reserve budgets and issue time-limited quotes. Quotes state the selected option, exact award and principal commitment. Acceptance executes the lock or confirms the staking award. Declined/expired quotes release reservations but do not retrospectively alter accepted awards or trigger automatic repricing. Monthly and lifetime accounting must count both outstanding reservations and accepted awards. No pre-funded lock is required merely to request a locking quote.

Approved extra rewards vest 60% / 20% / 20% from the approval month, independently of voluntary principal maturity. For example, 10,000 devices each earning 1,212.121212… MHA and each requesting a 12-month lock have 242.424242… MHA requests. With 500,000 MHA available and no other requests, each extra award is 50 MHA. This is an earned award, not 50 MHA immediately received.

Calculation basis: reward and vesting formulas

Device-scaled budget

Bₘ = (200,000,000 × 0.5^(y−1) / 12) × rampₘ
Cₘ = min(Bₘ × min(1, Nₘ / 13,750), Nₘ × 10,000)
Pₘ = Cₘ / Nₘ

y is the program year. Nₘ counts unique admitted, eligible GEN1 devices for the settlement period, not sales, shipments or wallet addresses. When Nₘ = 0, Cₘ, Pₘ and newly earned rewards are defined as zero without dividing by zero; previously earned batches retain their vesting schedule. Below 13,750 devices, the base mining budget scales down proportionally; at or above it, the budget stops growing and the per-device reference amount falls as participation increases. 13,750 is a budget baseline, not a sales target or device admission cap.

Availability and validated work

Eᵢ,ₘ = Pₘ × (0.20 × Aᵢ,ₘ + 0.80 × Wᵢ,ₘ)

A is the fraction of the entire settlement month during which a device is verified to meet service requirements and be ready to accept tasks. Powering on or connecting to the internet alone does not qualify. W is accepted, quality-adjusted useful work divided by the published full-month GEN1 reference capacity. Both range from 0 to 1, and W ≤ A. Completing every assigned task does not establish W = 1 when demand is low. No accepted tasks means W = 0. An eligible device with A = 1 and W = 0 can earn only the proposed 20% availability component. Invalid, duplicate or self-generated workload is excluded. Unused work rewards are not redistributed to exhaust the pool.

Split a month into non-overlapping subperiods j at eligibility changes. Let dⱼ = Δtⱼ / Tₘ, summing to 1 over the month. Each segment uses only Bₘ × dⱼ of the budget; Nⱼ is its unique eligible device count. A device earns nothing before admission or after departure; Nⱼ = 0 produces no new rewards.

Eᵢ,ₘ = Σⱼ [dⱼ × min(Bₘ / max(Nⱼ, 13,750), 10,000) × (0.20 × Aᵢ,ⱼ + 0.80 × Wᵢ,ⱼ)]

Aᵢ,ⱼ and Wᵢ,ⱼ use the full segment duration and corresponding reference work capacity, with 0 ≤ Wᵢ,ⱼ ≤ Aᵢ,ⱼ ≤ 1. Do not reuse full-month fractions inside this formula and prorate twice. Reference-capacity conversion, validation/capping of above-reference work and the settlement time zone must be published before launch. The base calculator supports constant counts within each month, not intramonth segments. Track each device and approved batch separately; current counts must not recalculate historical rewards.

Approved, unlocked and actually received

Vesting is indexed to the month in which a reward is earned. After approval, 40% is claimable and the remaining 60% vests in three equal installments, with the first assigned to the same month. The monthly model therefore releases 60%, then 20%, then 20%. Actual approval and claim dates must follow the launch announcement; task completion does not imply an immediate transfer.

Uᵢ,ₘ = 0.60 × Eᵢ,ₘ + 0.20 × Eᵢ,ₘ₋₁ + 0.20 × Eᵢ,ₘ₋₂

Released means scheduled to become claimable after settlement, not a verified transfer to a wallet. Actual receipt depends on completed verification, the published claim process and confirmed transfers. Unactivated devices earn nothing; this proposal applies no blanket 30% pre-activation payment factor or automatic later catch-up. No optional locking or staking reward multiplier is included in the schedule.

Eligibility and verification

Before activation, publish device registration, attestation and deduplication rules, availability sampling, accepted task types, reference benchmark (model, precision and hardware), quality thresholds, review/appeal procedures, claim timing and fees. One physical device must not earn duplicate rewards through multiple accounts. Shared networks alone do not prove abusive ownership. No tasks means no task reward.

Reward rates and applicable limits must be known before tasks are accepted. Reject invalid evidence and review unsettled rewards under published procedures; this draft does not establish a right or technical ability to seize tokens already transferred to a user wallet. Any bonded-stake slashing would require separately disclosed terms and implementation.

Activation and parameter changes

Launch requires disclosed benchmarks, a tested settlement ledger, an executable claim/vesting mechanism, sufficient reserved budget and an effective-date announcement. Review device admission, workload and budget use quarterly. Changes to the baseline, reward split or reference workload require a dated notice, documented authorization and a future effective period; changes must not retroactively rewrite earned batches. Governance and timelocks are not represented as deployed until verified.

Public reporting and methodology

Report eligible device counts, budget ceilings, availability and task rewards, unallocated reserve, earned batches, locked and claimable balances, actual transfers, rule version and exceptions. Budget, earned rewards, claimable rewards and on-chain transfers must remain separate measures.

Budget and vesting inputs originate from the project GEN1 tokenomics model; the 13,750-device baseline was selected for this draft on 1 October 2026. The separation of availability and useful-work rewards draws on Render RNP-021. The 13,750 baseline and 20% / 80% split are MAGNE proposal parameters, not Render parameters or an endorsement. No token price or monetary return is assumed.
