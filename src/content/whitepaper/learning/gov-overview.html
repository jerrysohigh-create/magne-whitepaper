Governance lifecycle — proposed parameters

Design status

The following retains earlier governance design parameters for review. They are not current operating instructions. MHA/mBGT voting rights and the voting-supply denominator require confirmation before these parameters can be adopted.

Proposed use cases

Protocol settings, reward-vault admission, application permissions and ecosystem budgets may be proposed within a published governance scope. Economic allocation changes require explicit proposals rather than being implied by an operational vote.

Lifecycle

  • Publish a proposal with purpose, affected contracts, funding source and executable actions.
  • Take the voting-power snapshot, apply the pending period and open voting.
  • Check quorum and the approval rule, then queue approved actions in the timelock.
  • Execute after the delay and disclose transaction results; emergency cancellation follows separately defined authority.

Guardian proposal

The earlier 5-of-9 guardian proposal would allow cancellation of specified dangerous actions during the timelock. Membership, appointment/removal, conflicts of interest and the exact cancellation scope remain to be published before activation.

Before a proposal can be submitted

Publish the governor and timelock addresses, supported interface, delegation method, quorum denominator, vote-counting rules and a worked execution example. Do not send funds to an address merely because it is labelled governance.
