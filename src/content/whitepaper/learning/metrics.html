Network metrics and measurement

Public configurations currently cover MAGNE L1 and M Hash L2 testnets. Mainnet and production settlement are not open. See the official network register for deployment status and parameters.

Metrics must identify the network, client/configuration version, observation period and workload. Earlier 1-second/2-second block times and 100M/140M gas limits were inconsistent; they are not presented here as verified current settings.

Throughput and fees

At 100M gas per second, standard transfers using 21,000 gas have a theoretical full-block ceiling of about 4,761 transactions per second, before other overhead. This calculation is not a measured MAGNE benchmark. An 8,000+ TPS claim requires a different documented workload or gas budget.

Benchmark reports should include transaction mix, hardware, duration, accepted and failed transactions, confirmation latency and the corresponding block range. Dollar-denominated fees vary with gas usage, fee parameters and token price; no fixed dollar fee or undated competitor comparison is asserted here.
