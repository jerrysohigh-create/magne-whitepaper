Governance — design and responsibilities

Governance is intended to make protocol changes and resource decisions reviewable. The roles below are a design proposal; this chapter does not establish that a governor, timelock or guardian committee is already deployed.

Decision scope

  • Protocol parameters, upgrades and the permissions needed to execute them.
  • Vault eligibility, reward budgets and ecosystem funding within the applicable allocation rules.
  • Publication of proposals, decisions, execution transactions and exceptions.

Operational roles

RoleProposed boundary
ManagerCarry out authorised operations; any emergency pause requires a defined scope and follow-up disclosure.
AuthorizerEnforce permissions between approved decisions and contract calls.
CoordinatorCoordinate execution order and dependencies; cancellation or delay powers must be explicit.

Voting rights and activation

The design references both MHA participation and mBGT reward coordination/protocol voting. Their relationship, delegation model and eligible voting supply must be confirmed together. Holding BSC MHA does not by itself demonstrate access to a live on-chain governor. Activation should publish the network, contract addresses, vote accounting, timelock and emergency permissions.
