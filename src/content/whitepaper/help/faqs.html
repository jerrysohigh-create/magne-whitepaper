Frequently asked questions

What is MHA?

Magic Hash (MHA) currently exists on BSC. Its total allocation is 10 billion. Planned roles in MAGNE-owned networks include gas, security staking and ecosystem incentives; migration terms require a separate announcement.

Does MAGNE use a second governance token?

The design references mBGT, but its retention and relationship to MHA voting rights remain to be confirmed. This draft neither declares a final single-token governance model nor announces a new token issuance.

How fast is M Hash L2?

Use dated measurements for the stated network and workload. Earlier 1-second and 2-second claims were not reconciled. Block inclusion, settlement finality and bridge withdrawal time are different metrics. Measurement guide

Is a phone owner automatically a validator?

No. Device tasks, validator admission and L2 ordering have separate rules. GEN1 eligibility does not grant block-production rights.

What are the proposed validator requirements?

The earlier proposal specified 250,000–10,000,000 MHA per validator, suitable server resources and availability checks. Admission, selection, penalties and the final consensus model require a confirmed technical specification before activation.

Where do validator rewards come from?

The 15% Staking Nodes allocation is separate from the 30% Mining Nodes allocation. Its earlier schedule releases half of that pool in year one and halves the annual amount thereafter. Eligibility and weighting must follow its own final rules. Staking allocation

Does a phone receive 20,000 MHA in any 24 months?

No. The illustration assumes participation from network M1, a fixed eligible population no greater than 13,750, full availability/effective work, and the standard base release schedule. Late entry and changing device counts change the result.

What is PoL?

Proof of Liquidity is a proposed way to coordinate eligible liquidity participation and reward allocation. It must not be treated as a complete consensus specification by itself. The deployed vault rules determine eligibility, release and claims.

Do liquidity deposits automatically earn rewards?

Not necessarily. Pool fees and additional vault incentives are separate. Vault admission, eligible receipt assets, funded budget and claim requirements must be checked for the actual deployment.

What is a DEX or liquidity pool?

A DEX uses smart contracts to support asset exchange. A liquidity pool holds assets under its contract rules; price impact depends on pool design, depth and trade size. Swap fees, network gas and slippage are different costs.

How do APR and APY differ?

APR is an annualised rate without compounding; APY includes an assumed compounding schedule. Neither is a guaranteed return. Fee income, incentives and asset-value changes must be distinguished.

Can holders vote now?

This draft does not establish a live governor. The voting asset, delegation method, contract and start date must be published before voting instructions apply. Governance proposal
